# Hardware Research

This directory contains research related to hardware support and integration for Theseus OS.

## Target Devices

*   **Xiaomi Redmi Note 7 (lavender)**: Detailed research on its components, existing driver support, and potential challenges.
*   **Future Architectures**: Research into ARM, RISC-V, and other architectures.

## Key Components

*   **CPU**: Architecture-specific features and requirements.
*   **GPU**: Driver interfaces, Vulkan/OpenGL support.
*   **Peripherals**: Modems, cameras, storage controllers, displays, etc.

## Research Areas

*   **Hardware Identification**: Robust methods for detecting and classifying hardware.
*   **Device Tree**: Understanding and utilizing device tree descriptors.
*   **Driver Models**: Modular and portable driver design.
